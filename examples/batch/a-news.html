Harbour town opens new ferry terminal

The new ferry terminal opened on Monday, giving commuters a covered waiting area and a second berth for the larger boats that now serve the outer islands.

Officials said the project took three years and cost slightly less than the original estimate. Most of the savings came from reusing the old pier pilings instead of driving new ones.

Ferry operators expect the extra berth to cut waiting times in the summer months, when queues at the old landing often reached the car park.

Residents who spoke at the opening welcomed the shelter and the step-free access to the platform, which had been a long-standing request from local groups.
