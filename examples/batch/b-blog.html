Notes from a week of bread baking

This week I baked a loaf every morning, changing one thing at a time: the hydration, the length of the bulk ferment, and the temperature of the oven.

The wetter dough was harder to shape but opened up in the crumb, with larger holes near the crust. The longer ferment gave a sour note I liked more than I expected.

The best loaf came on Friday, when I kept the dough cool overnight and baked it straight from the fridge. The crust blistered evenly and the base did not burn.

Next week I plan to try a rye starter, which needs its own feeding schedule and a much shorter proof.
